Reinforced concrete beam: bending reinforcement (EN 1992-1-1)

Tension reinforcement of a rectangular reinforced concrete beam in bending with the simplified rectangular stress block, and the bars to provide.

A 300 × 450 mm effective section in C30/37 carries 150 kN·m. The concrete strength comes from the concrete table, the area of five 16 mm bars from the rebar table.

Valid while K ≤ 0.167, when no compression reinforcement is needed.
