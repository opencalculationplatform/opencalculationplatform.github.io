Pipe flow: Reynolds number and pressure drop

Flow velocity, Reynolds number, Darcy friction factor (Swamee–Jain) and pressure drop of water in a pipe.

12 L/s of water through 50 m of 100 mm pipe. The friction factor uses the Swamee–Jain approximation of the Colebrook equation; OCP's log is base 10.
