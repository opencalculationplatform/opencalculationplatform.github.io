Simply supported beam: moment, shear and deflection

Bending moment, support shear and mid-span deflection of a simply supported beam under a uniform load, with the IPE section's Iy taken from a table.

A steel floor beam spans 6 m and carries 10 kN/m. Its second moment of area comes straight from the IPE table; OCP keeps the units, so the deflection comes out in millimetres.

The last line is a check: 1 means it holds. Change the section to "IPE 270" or the load, and every line updates.

Try it in OCP. Type these formulas in a new worksheet, change any value, and every result updates, with its unit. Download OCP, free.
