Heat loss through a wall: U-value

Thermal transmittance (U-value) of a layered wall and the heat flow through it for a temperature difference.

100 mm of insulation and 200 mm of brick, with the usual inside and outside surface resistances. OCP adds the resistances with their units and gives U in W/(m²·K).
