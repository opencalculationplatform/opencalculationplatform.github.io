Free ways to help

  • Tell a colleague about OCP, or share the website.
  • Publish a component or table to the directory.
  • Report a problem or suggest a feature.
  • Star OCP on GitHub.

What your support pays for

  • Development time: new features from the roadmap and fixes, sooner.
  • Publishing costs: the Google Play account, code signing so Windows trusts the installer, a web address.
  • Keeping OCP free: the core of OCP stays free for everyone, and early-access Pro features too.

Support is a gift: it does not buy a licence or a service, and OCP works the same with or without it. Thank you.
