Roadmap

Where OCP is going

OCP grows with the people who use it. Here is what has shipped, what we are working on now, and what comes after. Plans change with your feedback: suggest a feature or report a problem.

Now

In progress

OCP on Google Play

Install and update the Android app from the Play Store, instead of the APK.

More in the directory

More tables and ready-made checks for every discipline: mechanical, electrical, fluids, thermal and structural.

Next

Planned

Open-source calculation engine

OCP's engine (formulas, units, solvers, symbolic math) published as open source, so anyone can check how results are worked out.

Plugins for developers

A ready-made project template and documentation for writing OCP plugins in C#: new functions, calculation sections and components.

Dark theme

OCP in dark colours on the phone, following the phone's setting.

Your own web address

A short, easy-to-share address for the website.

Later

Ideas we are exploring

AI help

Describe a check in words, and OCP drafts the worksheet or component for you to review.

Python on the phone

Python functions and scripts on Android, as on Windows.

iPhone and iPad

OCP on iOS, with the same worksheets.

OCP Pro

OCP stays free. Advanced features marked Pro (Word export, Excel sheets, report contents) are free for everyone during early access; a paid Pro edition may come later.

Released

Recently shipped

0.8
  • PDF reports on the phone: see the report, then save it as a PDF or print it.
  • Pictures on the phone: take a photo or choose one, straight into the worksheet.
  • Find and replace on the phone, as on Windows.
0.7
  • Automatic updates: OCP tells you when a new version is out and, on Windows, updates itself with one click (the download is checked before it is installed). You can turn it off.
  • Cleaner worksheets: a formula with an error has just a red frame; its message shows when the mouse is over it, or when you tap it on the phone.
  • Reports print formulas such as solve(…= 0, x) → whole. Support OCP from About.
0.6.1
  • Tables from Excel workbooks, and a viewer for every table.
  • About on the phone.
  • Fixes: worksheets with some directory components reopen after saving; the solver shows an equation equal to 0 as holding.
0.6
  • Python functions (Windows): your own functions in Python, with units, in any formula.
  • The directory: install components and tables shared by OCP users, and publish yours with one click.
0.5
  • Components: any worksheet becomes a calculation block with a form and a report, without code; worked examples; components inside components.
  • Tables as functions: IPE("IPE 300", "A"), with a drop-down of a table's rows.
  • Pro features marked, free during early access.
0.4 and earlier
  • The Android app: OCP's keypad, live answers, column view, touch editing.
  • Worksheets with units, plots, solvers, symbolic math, PDF and Word reports, and opening Mathcad Prime files.
