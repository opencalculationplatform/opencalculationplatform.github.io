OCP Guide

Units

Write units after numbers and OCP converts everything, shows each result in a sensible unit, and warns you when the units do not add up.

On this pageNumbers with unitsMixing unitsUS customary unitsOCP checks your unitsChoosing the unit systemYour own unitsUnits OCP knows

Numbers with units

Type the unit after the number: 6 m, 12 kN/m, 355 MPa. OCP keeps track of what each quantity is, so a moment comes out in kN·m and a stress in MPa.

To show a result in a particular unit, type the unit after =: M = kN·cm.

Choosing the unit system

Format › Units… picks the unit system (SI, US customary or CGS) and, for each quantity, the unit results use: lengths in mm, stresses in MPa, moments in kN·m. On the phone: ⋮ › Format and page.

Your own units

Any name you define can act as a unit: write it after a number, or after = to show a result in it.
