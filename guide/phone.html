OCP Guide

The phone app

OCP on Android: a keypad made for engineering formulas, live answers, column view for narrow screens, and touch gestures for everything.

On this pageStart screenThe keypadLive answers, Ans and HistoryPage view and column viewTouchFiles

Start screen

OCP opens on a blank worksheet with a formula ready. ☰ opens the start screen: New calculation, Open worksheet…, Recent worksheets (× removes one) and Continue. OCP asks before leaving unsaved changes. Back returns to the start screen, then leaves the app.

The keypad

  • 123: digits, ÷ × − +, brackets, powers xⁿ and x², √, Ans, := and =, ⌫ and ⏎.
  • SCI: sin, cos, tan and their inverses, ln, log, eˣ, π, e, degrees, x⁻¹, x³, ∛, ⁿ√, |x|, %, ×10ⁿ.
  • ABC: letters (AZERTY for French, otherwise QWERTY), subscript, space. ⌨ there switches to your phone's keyboard; the blue Keypad button brings OCP's back.
  • αβ: Greek letters. kN: units by quantity.
  • The bar above has ◀ ▶ (move the cursor; slide along them for fine moves), → (symbolic), x₁ (subscript), f(x), ∫, comparisons, Text, Plot, Solve, undo and redo.

Live answers, Ans and History

While you type a plain calculation, its answer shows faded beside it; = makes it real. Ans inserts the last answer at full precision. History lists your recent answers, newest first; tap one to insert it.

Page view and column view

⋮ › Column view fits any worksheet to the phone: formulas side by side become rows in reading order, and plots and pictures scale to the width. ⋮ › Format and page › Text and formula size sets how large they are. Page view shows the real A4 page. Your choice is remembered.

Touch

  • Tap a formula to edit it, with the cursor where you tapped.
  • Hold a region and lift to open its menu (Copy result, Disable, Select…); hold and drag to move it.
  • Select in that menu starts selecting: tap more regions, then Copy, Cut or Delete. Paste on the bar puts them back.
  • Pinch to zoom; the formula you type stays in view.
  • Drag the round blue grip of a plot or picture to resize it.
  • Selecting a number shows Android's own Copy bar; a copied fraction pastes back as a fraction.
  • The S Pen works like a finger, and more precisely.

Files

⋮ › Open, Save as and Share use Android's file picker and share sheet, so worksheets can live anywhere: Downloads, Drive, or sent by email. Worksheets open the same on Windows and on the phone.
