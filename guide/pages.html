Text, layout and organisation

Headings and text, formatting, sections, protected areas, hyperlinks, find and replace, and the page itself.

On this pageText and headingsFormattingSectionsProtected sectionsHyperlinksFind and replaceThe page

Text and headings

Insert › Text, or type " in an empty formula, starts a text region. Heading 1 and Heading 2 structure the worksheet; they become the report's contents and numbered sections.

Text runs right to left automatically for Persian or Arabic; Format › Right to left sets it by hand.

Formatting

  • Format › Font: bold, italic, underline, colour and highlight (a colour behind any text or formula).
  • Format › All formulas: the font and size of every formula.
  • Format › Styles: Normal, Heading 1, Heading 2.
  • Format › Results: number format, digits, trailing zeros (0.5000) and Values in formulas.

Sections

Select regions and choose Section: they get a bar with a − button that folds them away. Folded regions still calculate. Use them to tuck away detail such as intermediate steps.

Protected sections

Select a section and choose Math › Protect: what it covers cannot be edited, moved or deleted until you press it again, and Replace all skips it. A guard for checked work in a template (not a password).

Hyperlinks

Insert › Hyperlink links the selected regions to a web page, a file (another worksheet, a PDF, a drawing, an Office document) or a heading of this worksheet (#Loads). Ctrl+click follows it.

Find and replace

Ctrl+F finds, Ctrl+H replaces. Names are matched whole in formulas, so searching M does not find M_Ed, and renaming a variable renames every use in one undoable step. In text, choose match case and whole words.

The page

  • Page Layout: paper size, portrait or landscape, and margins in mm.
  • Insert › Page break starts a new page at the insertion point.
  • Regions snap to a grid; drag them by their border. Select several with a drag, or Ctrl+A for all.
