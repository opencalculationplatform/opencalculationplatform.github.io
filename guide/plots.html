Plots, sketches and pictures

Charts, engineering diagrams, bars, polar and contour plots, 3D surfaces, sketches whose sizes are formulas, and pictures.

On this pagePlotsSketchesPictures

Plots

Insert › Plot (phone: Plot on the bar). A new plot takes the formula you were typing or last edited: f(x) := … is plotted from 0 to 10, which you can change. Plots update whenever their inputs change.

In the plot editor (Edit plot):

  • What is plotted: functions of a variable from a start to an end, vectors of data (x and y), parametric curves (x(t), y(t)), or a surface z(x, y). Several series in one plot, each with a label.
  • Style: chart, engineering diagram (filled, with the positive side below, the maximum marked), bars, polar, contour map or 3D surface.
  • Axes: titles, units, limits and reference lines. Units on the axes follow the worksheet's.
  • A plot over a range variable uses the range's points.

Drag the corner to resize a plot. 3D surfaces turn with the mouse or a finger.

Sketches

Insert › Sketch draws lines, arrows, dimensions, rectangles, circles, triangles, supports and text whose positions, sizes and labels can be formulas: L, L/2, {w}. Change the span and the drawing follows. Snapping follows the shapes.

Pictures

Insert › Picture adds a PNG, JPEG or GIF, kept inside the worksheet. You can also drop a picture on the page or paste one with Ctrl+V.
