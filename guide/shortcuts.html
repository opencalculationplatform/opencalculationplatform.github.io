Keyboard shortcuts

Work faster on Windows: the keys for files, editing and typing formulas.

On this pageFiles and calculationEditingIn a formula

Files and calculation

Ctrl+NNew worksheet
Ctrl+OOpen
Ctrl+S / Ctrl+Shift+SSave / Save As
Ctrl+WClose the worksheet
F9Calculate now (when Automatic is off)

Editing

Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+YUndo / Redo
Ctrl+ASelect every region
Ctrl+C, Ctrl+X, Ctrl+VCopy, cut, paste (a picture too)
DeleteDelete the selected regions
Ctrl+DeleteRemove the region being edited
Ctrl+EDisable or enable the selected regions
Ctrl+F / Ctrl+HFind / Replace
Ctrl+clickFollow a hyperlink

In a formula

::= definition
~≡ global definition
=show the result
/ ^ _ *fraction, power, subscript, multiplication
→ ←leave a power, subscript or fraction; move
..range 1..10
" in an empty formulaturn it into text
Entera new formula below
Taba new formula to the right
a name such as alpha, sqrt, sum, intα, √, Σ, ∫

View › Shortcuts… lists every typing shortcut; you can change, remove or add your own. View › Formula shortcuts turns them off.
