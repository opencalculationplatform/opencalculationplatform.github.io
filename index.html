Why engineers like it

Your calculation note, without the busywork

No cells, no code, no copying numbers from a calculator. Just formulas you can read, checked as you write them.

Live math with units

Type it as you think it. Get the answer with its unit.

Write M := q·L²/8 and the result appears at once, in kN·m. Mix millimetres and metres, kN and N: OCP converts everything and tells you when the units do not add up.

  • Results update the moment an input changes, through the whole sheet.
  • Diagrams follow your formulas: change the span, the moment diagram redraws.
  • Pick a section from a drop-down and every check below follows.
On your phone

A real calculator for engineers, in your pocket.

The Android app opens the same worksheets as your PC. Its keypad has fractions, powers, roots, units and Greek letters a tap away, with the answer shown live as you type.

  • Check a calculation on site, change a value, see every result update.
  • Column view fits any worksheet to a narrow screen.
  • Open, save and share worksheets like any document; works with the S Pen.
Plots, exact math and solvers

From a quick plot to a 3D surface. From x to the exact answer.

Charts, diagrams, bars, polar and contour plots, and 3D surfaces you can turn. Exact answers for integrals, limits and simplifications. A solver that finds the root, says how it found it, and checks the answer for you.

  • Matrices, complex numbers and ranges, with units.
  • Solve one equation or a system; optimise; fit data.
  • Every solver answer is checked back in its equation.
Components

Write a check once. Reuse it everywhere, without code.

Save any worksheet as a component: a calculation block with a form for its inputs and a clear report of every step. Drop it into the next project, fill in the form, done.

  • Each step shown as written math, with the inputs in your units.
  • Worked examples are recalculated every time, so a broken change is caught.
  • Components can use other components.
Tables

Your section and material tables, inside your formulas.

Bring in a CSV or Excel sheet and ask it for a value: IPE("IPE 300", "Wpl_y") is 628.4 cm³, with its unit, ready for the next formula. Choose a row from a drop-down and the whole check follows.

  • IPE, HEA and HEB sections, concrete and steel grades and rebar areas are ready in the directory.
  • See every row and column inside OCP.
The directory

Checks and tables shared by engineers, one tap away.

Browse the open directory from inside OCP and install what you need: section catalogues, material classes, beam, column and footing checks. Share yours with one click.

  • Everything is reviewed before it is listed.
  • Nothing you install runs code: components are worksheets, tables are data.
  • Each download is checked against its fingerprint.

Questions

Good to know

Is OCP really free?

Yes. OCP has no account, no ads and no trial period. Some advanced features are marked Pro (Word export, Excel sheets, report contents); during early access they are free for everyone.

Does it work without the internet?

Yes. Everything runs on your device. OCP only goes online when you open the directory or publish to it. See the privacy policy.

Can I open my Mathcad worksheets?

OCP opens Mathcad Prime files (.mcdx): formulas, text, units, plots and more.

Windows says it doesn't know the app. Is it safe?

OCP is new, so Windows SmartScreen may not recognise it yet. Choose More info › Run anyway to install. The setup installs for your user only and needs no administrator rights.

How do I install the Android app?

Download the APK on your phone and open it. Android asks once to allow your browser to install apps; allow it, then install. A Google Play version is coming.

Which units and standards does it know?

SI, US customary and CGS units, with conversions between them, and your own units. The directory has Eurocode tables and checks (EN 1992, EN 1993, EN 10365), and anyone can add more.
